Homework Tab + Upload & AI Digitize Flow

Revised per Tony's Figma comments on this flow: one big rectangle CTA (not a chooser screen) — tap it and the camera opens directly, "Choose from Library" lives as a small icon next to the photo instead of a separate button, and manual entry is a de-emphasized text link below. The due-date step before digitizing is removed entirely; due date is now asked on the Review screen, first and biggest. Scanning copy is now "fun and friendly" per his note. Detail view adds the approved "See Original Photo" + "Edit" buttons, and Edit routes back into the same form pre-filled. Sample content is the real "ME Bag" sheet; the second row is placeholder content for the overdue state. New from the 8/26 sync: per Mohit, the digitized rules/suggestions text is now directly editable (not just the title) so a misread photo can be fixed by hand before saving; a scan can also fail (blurry photo) and routes to a friendly error screen with Retake/Enter Manually — try the flow twice to see both the failure and the retry succeeding. Per Tony, there's also a Homework/Document type toggle above Due Date, since not everything coming home is homework. New from Tony's 8/25 Figma comment: the Type toggle and Due Date no longer wait for the Review screen — they're right there on the scanning screen itself, so there's something to do during the "AI is reading it" wait instead of staring at a spinner. Whatever's set there carries straight into Review — and if you answered the due date there, Review shows it as a compact confirmation chip ("Edit" to change it) instead of asking the same two questions again; only skip it during the wait and Review still asks in full.

Rules to follow
  • Only bring things that fit inside the bag.
  • Items shouldn't be too heavy — you should be able to carry it yourself.
  • Only bring items that can be left at school for the day of sharing.
  • Bring no more than 5 items.
  • Do not bring very special or breakable items.
Suggestions
  • Something in your favorite color
  • The wrapper of your favorite snack
  • An award you have won
  • Something that tells about your favorite sport/animal/etc.
  • Something from a trip
  • Photos of your family or pet
  • Something you made
