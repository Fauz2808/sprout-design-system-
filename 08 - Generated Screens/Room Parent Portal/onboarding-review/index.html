Sign in and onboarding already exist

Seven screens, all built, all in the prototype today. These are screenshots of the live build, not mockups. Nothing here needs designing, only porting.

Live now Walk it yourself: room-parent-portal.vercel.app Type any 10 digits that are not (512) 555-0147, then any 6 digits as the code. An unknown number is what opens onboarding.
What the real portal does today instead. On cms.roomparent.com the sign-in screen is built, from this design, word for word. The five onboarding screens are not. After a correct code, an account that is not finished gets this: “Your account setup isn't complete — finish your profile in the Sprout app, then sign in again.” A room parent with no Sprout app has nowhere to go from there, which is the gap Tony raised on 15 Sep.

The whole thing, left to right

The green panel is identical on every screen, so it is shown once in full and cropped away after that. Ids are the .auth-layout ids in index.html.

1 · auth-phoneSign in The whole layout, once. Phone number only, no password anywhere in the flow. An unknown number is what routes into onboarding instead of the dashboard. Blocks on: not exactly 10 digits.
2 · auth-codeEnter your code Six boxes, paste-aware, backspace walks back a box. Verify stays disabled until all six are filled. Blocks on: fewer than 6 digits.
3 · auth-profileYour profile Photo, first and last name, zip, and a Mom or Dad tile. Onboarding starts here, the moment the number is not on the roster. Blocks on: missing name, missing zip, or no role picked.
4 · auth-interestsActivities The real activity list from the app's own Select Activities screen, not placeholders. Capped at 10. Blocks on: none picked.
5 · auth-kidsYour children Name, grade, gender, photo per child. Adding a row is not a saved thing yet, so removing one asks nothing. Blocks on: no children added.
6 · auth-schoolsSchool and grades Schools collapsed by default, sorted by distance from the zip given two screens ago. Grades are multi-select, because one parent can have children in three of them. Blocks on: no school, or a school with no grade.
7 · auth-classgroupThe class group The only screen with real logic. It checks whether a class already exists for that school and grade, and if so offers to join it, with how many room parents it already has, instead of letting you create a duplicate. Picking one disables the name field. Blocks on: no grade, or a new class with no name. This check must also exist on the server.
