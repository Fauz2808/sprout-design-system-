Compose Email on mobile — three directions

Same content, same tokens, three different structures. All three are live: tap through them. The point is to choose a structure, not to judge polish.

Already decided, applied to all three: Previously Sent is out of the send flow · recipients are chosen at the end, after the preview, the way Tony asked for twice · nothing is sent until Send is pressed, so no direction saves a partial draft to the backend between steps.
Direction A

Wizard

Five full-screen steps with Next, Back and Skip. Literally what Tony and Mohit described in the sync.

Each screen is short, and required fields gate the next step
Guides a room parent sending their first class email
A one-paragraph email still costs five screens
Changing one word at the review step means going back three
Direction B

Collapsed blocksRecommended

One page. Each block collapses to a summary row and opens in place. Preview and Send live in the bar at the bottom.

5,108px becomes 616px: measured, everything fits with no scrolling at all
Jump to any block in any order; edit after preview is one tap
No navigation at all, so nothing to save between steps
Less hand-holding on a first send than a wizard
Direction C

Preview first

The finished email is the screen. Tap any part of it to edit that part in a sheet.

The scroll-down-to-preview problem disappears entirely
Matches how Tony thinks: on To-Do he said "I'm looking at the preview right now"
Most work to build, and the most new interaction for Mohit
Tap targets on a rendered email need care to stay obvious
Try this in each: open with two template fields empty, then get to Send. That is the real first-use path, and it is where the three structures feel most different.
