Ahmad's honest read, for the discussion with Tony

Functionally almost identical. Same fields, same end state, same screens reused — the only real change is when it's asked. It's not a redesign, it's a resequencing.

What it buys: sign-up drops from 4 required steps to 3, and the tap count to reach Home drops by roughly half (no search → expand → pick grade → continue chain up front).

What it risks: the current version guarantees every kid gets mapped to a school group before they ever see the rest of the app — that mapping is what powers school-based matching (class updates, nearby-parent discovery) from day one. Move it to a dismissible "maybe later" card, and some parents will dismiss it and never come back, weakening that matching for exactly the users who skipped it.

Given onboarding is already long, this trims one blocking step — but it trades a guarantee for a hope. Worth Tony's call on whether that trade is right for where Sprout is right now.
