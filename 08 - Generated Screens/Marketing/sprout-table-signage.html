Why this works
"Hi, parent." The most disarming opener possible. Names the audience instantly, sounds like a person not a brand. Parents walking with kids will recognize themselves in three seconds — exactly the time you have.
"We made you something. Help yourself." Reframes the table from "marketing booth" to "gift." No ask, no pitch, no signup. The merch is the message; the message is welcome.
The FREE stamp earns its space because at distance, "FREE" is the single most important word. The yellow circle pops against green and signals "no catch" before the parent even reads the headline.
The list at the bottom is the same triad from the yard sign — building brand consistency across touchpoints. Repetition is recognition.

Tent design notes
Side A handles the offer: "Take one. It's yours." removes any uncertainty about whether they need to talk to someone, sign up, or pay. "No signup. No catch." preempts the default parent skepticism at any school-adjacent booth.
Side B handles the brand: Same three-line value prop from the foam board, in case the parent picks something up and wants to know what Sprout actually is before walking away.
Why two sides matter: Tents on table edges are visible from two directions — the side you're standing on and the side facing the table. Each side serves a different role rather than duplicating the same message.
Production & Setup

Printing & logistics

📋 Foam Board — 24×36"
White foam board with full-bleed print, mounted to a folding tabletop easel ($15–25 on Amazon). GotPrint or PrintRunner: 3–5 days for ~$25–40 per board. Order 3–4 so you always have spares.
🗂 Table Tent — 6×8" folded
16pt cardstock or thicker. VistaPrint, Moo, or PrintRunner all stock this format. ~$2–4 per piece at low volumes. Print 6–10 per table so you can scatter them around the merch piles.
📐 Table Setup Ratio
1 foam board behind the table on easel. 2–3 tents on the table surface — one at each merch grouping. Keep it simple; too much signage looks like a sales pitch.
⏱ Lead Time
Allow 7–10 days for first run if doing school pickup events. Reorder takes 3–5 days once you have the print files. Order before the first event — don't scramble at the last minute.
