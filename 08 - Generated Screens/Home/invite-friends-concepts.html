Inviting friends to Sprout

Tony, 9 Oct: "For the parent that's super connected at a school, how do we allow them to invite their friends, even friends from other schools?" R1 (7 Oct) already decided where share lives: a card at the end of the brief, and once after the spouse connects. These are about when to ask and why a parent would. Decided 9 Oct: A, B and C together, as one loop.

Decided (Ahmad, 9 Oct):
  1. Share and rating together in one sheet on day 7 (A).
  2. No limit on invites. Every parent has one personal link and uses it as often as they like (B).
  3. The reward is getting Sprout Assist first: 3 parents who join from your link and you're in the beta (C).
  4. The link drops the friend into the inviter's class. No app yet: the link goes to the store first, and the first time the app opens (after sign-up) it goes straight into that class (B3, B4).

Never in onboarding (Tony: "I don't like the app yet"). Nothing parents already use is ever locked; Sprout stays free for parents, always. Order to build: A ships with the next release (no backend); B and C need Mohit's personal links and the install handoff (see the note at the bottom).

For Mohit: what this needs

  1. One personal link per parent (joinsprout.co/i/<handle>), and a record of who joined from whose link. That record powers B1's faces, B5's notification and C's "2 of 3".
  2. The install handoff (deferred deep link). The friend taps the link, installs from the store, signs up, and the app must still know which class they picked. Android has the Play Install Referrer for this; iOS has nothing built in, so it needs a service such as Branch or AppsFlyer OneLink (Firebase Dynamic Links shut down in August 2025). If the app is already installed, the link opens it directly (universal / app links).
  3. The web page in B3 lists the inviter's classes from that link. The same page as today's class join link, plus the inviter's face and the class picker.
  4. Day-7 sheet (A): a 7-day timer from first use, shown once; "Rate Sprout" calls the system review prompt (SKStoreReviewController / Play In-App Review). No backend.

Still open

  1. A friend from another school: should "Find yours" still credit the inviter toward Assist (C)? Proposed: yes, any join from the link counts.
  2. Who gets Assist when the inviter is in: just the inviter, or the family (Tony and Lydia)? Proposed: the family, since Assist is shared.
