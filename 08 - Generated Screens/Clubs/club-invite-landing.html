Club invite — the page you land on

Rebuilt against Tony's four comments on the Figma. Same flow the event invite already uses: verify your number on the web, the app is offered rather than required.

The rule his comments add up to: only show what the club creation flow guarantees. A club always has a name, an icon, a description (required at creation) and at least one member, its creator. Everything else — a school, an area, a calendar — may simply not exist, so none of it can be load-bearing.
Added after the 15 Sep call. Tony: "I don't know what happens after request to join. I think it's you just add your phone number and then it sends a code, with the Sprout download thing." Steps 3 and 4 are that code screen and that handoff, plus the two endings the flow actually splits into and the one it had no ending for at all.
