Manage & Discover Schools / Grades

Tony's ask: onboarding only lets you pick one school + grade — there's no way after that to add another, leave one, switch, or browse what else is out there. Subgroups already have this fully (search, join/request, create — see subgroup-hub-final.html), grades and schools didn't. This is that same approved pattern, one level up, rendered in the current icon/token system rather than that file's pre-standardization styling.

Entry point: Account → "Schools & Grades" — same place as Notifications/Privacy, since this is account-level structure, not something you'd bury inside a chat. No existing Settings screen to hang it on yet, so a minimal one is included here to show the real tap-through.
Try it: tap a grade to open Manage → Update Grades — one universal checklist for every grade at that school. Check or uncheck any of them; it's not framed as switching from one to another, since half the time you're just adding a second kid's grade, not leaving the first.
Hit Update and you'll only see a confirmation if something's actually changing — it calls out which grades you're leaving and warns that their subgroups won't carry over. No-op taps (nothing checked/unchecked) skip the confirmation entirely.
Land back on Manage for a newly-added grade and its subgroups are now live — join or leave one right from the list, tap a subgroup to see its members.
From the hub, "Add a school or grade" still searches everything — existing schools' other grades, or a brand new school entirely. That path is unchanged.
From Manage, Leave still works like a subgroup leave: confirmed, not destructive, rejoinable — a grade can never be deleted, only left.
