Room Parent Portal — Mobile Nav, 2 Arah

8 item nav (Dashboard, Compose Email, Parents, Calendar, Links, Fundraising, Consent Forms, Room Parent To-Do) kepanjangan buat 1 baris bottom-bar mobile — sekarang overflow, harus di-scroll horizontal buat ketemu item di ujung.

"Also this should be in a hamburger menu vs a footer that you have to scroll to find the other things. Or we need to make it all fit below." — Tony, review room-parent-portal.vercel.app
Concept A

Hamburger → full nav drawer

Bottom bar diciutin jadi 4 slot penting (Dashboard, Email, Calendar, Fundraising) + 1 slot Menu. Tap Menu buka drawer isi semua 8 item, full label, satu tap langsung sampai — ga ada yang perlu di-scroll buat ketemu.
