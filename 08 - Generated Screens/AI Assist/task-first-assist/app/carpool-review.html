Carpool, in Tony’s order

One time or regular, then the date (regular: the days and until when) and one pickup time, never a range. Pick up from and drop off at come from what Sprout registered (Home, School, Soccer, Summer camp, or an address). One way or both ways. Then the families nearby whose kids do the same activity, and the whole group chat or parents you pick.

  • One time: the first yes drives, the others are told, nobody confirms twice
  • Regular: everyone who says yes gets turns; tap a turn to change who drives
  • Parent tiles are the same as Playdate’s
