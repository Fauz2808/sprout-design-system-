Date night, live on the lock screen

Once a night is booked, Sprout pushes both parents 30 minutes before the sitter arrives and keeps the evening on the lock screen as a Live Activity until you’re home. Drag the time, or press play. Sample night: Olive & Ember at 7:15, Alex 6:45–10:15.

  • Both parents get the push and the Live Activity: it’s a family plan (rule 2 on the wiring map).
  • Push at 30 minutes before the first thing: the sitter’s arrival, or dinner if you said “we’re covered”.
  • The Live Activity starts with that push and ends 15 minutes after the sitter’s end time (Apple caps them at 8 hours, this night is 4).
  • Buttons do one thing each: Text Alex opens Messages to Alex, Directions opens Maps. Nothing is sent by itself.
  • Running late: from 9:45 the button becomes “Ask Alex for 30 more min”, a prefilled text.
