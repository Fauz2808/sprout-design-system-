Both sides of a request

When Sprout asks someone for you (a playdate, a ride, a sitter), this is what they get. On each row: what you do on the left, what the other parent or sitter sees on the right. Their phone starts on the lock screen and opens by itself; tap an answer to see what comes back.

01

Playdate

Tony invites Lily’s parents, Ana and Ben. They’re in Sprout, so the invite lands in their chat with Tony as a card they can answer.

Tony

Picks the time, the kid and the place, then the parent.

→Sprout
sends it

Ana, Lily’s mom

Gets the invite in her chat with Tony.

How it connects

  1. Tony taps Send on the invite.
  2. Ana sees it in her chat with Tony: Confirm, Suggest another time, Can’t make it or Reply.
  3. Whatever she taps comes back to Tony’s plan. Confirm puts it on both families’ calendars.

Priya, Leo’s mom

Gets a push, opens Jake’s team chat, sees the ride card.

How it connects

  1. Tony taps Post in Jake’s team chat.
  2. Every parent on the team gets a push. Priya opens it and taps I can drive.
  3. Tony sees Priya drives Jake, it goes on both calendars, and the card tells everyone else it’s taken.

How it connects

  1. Tony taps Ask Priya and Aisha. Sprout starts one group text with each.

04

Babysitter · group text

Sitters don’t have Sprout. Each sitter gets a group text from Sprout with Tony on it, so Tony sees every reply and never sends anything himself.

Tony

Date and time, kids, the request, then “Ask Alex, Sam and Riley”.

→Group text
Tony + Sprout

Alex, the sitter

A normal text, no app to download.

How it connects

  1. Tony taps Ask Alex, Sam and Riley. Each sitter gets their own group text.
  2. Alex replies Yes, I’m free. Tony taps Book Alex in Sprout.
  3. Sprout sends Alex the details in the same text, tells Sam it’s covered, and Tony pays Alex in Sprout after.

Prototype: nothing is sent. Names, times and replies are samples, and they match on both sides of each row.
