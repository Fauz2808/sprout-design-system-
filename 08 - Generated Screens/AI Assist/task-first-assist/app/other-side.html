Review page · not part of the app

Both sides of a request

When Sprout asks someone for you (a playdate, a ride, a sitter, a class group), this is what they get. On each row the two phones are connected: do it on Tony’s phone on the left, and the person he asks gets it on the right. Their answer, and anything they type, goes back to Tony. Start over on either phone resets both.

Playdate

Live: the two phones are connected. Plan it on Tony’s phone; the parent he picks gets it on the right, in their chat with Tony, and whatever they tap goes back to Tony.

Tony

The parent Tony picks

Waits on the lock screen until Tony sends the invite.

How it connects

  1. Tony picks the kid, time and place, then a parent, say Ana and Ben. Sprout messages them.
  2. Their phone gets the push and opens their chat with Tony: Confirm, Suggest another time, Can’t make it or Reply.
  3. Confirm puts it on both calendars. A new time comes back to Tony to Accept or answer with another. Can’t lets Tony ask another family. Anything typed shows on both phones.
  4. Start over on either phone resets both.

Babysitter · ask a class group

Tony has no sitters saved, so Sprout posts his ask in the class chat. Every side of it is its own phone here: a parent who has the chat open, a parent who only gets the push, and the sitter they share, who has no Sprout. Numbers go to Tony only, never into the chat.

Tony

Book a sitter, then “Ask a class group” and Maya’s class. Picks from the sitters parents share, then Book.

A parent in the class chat

Priya, Leo’s mom, has the class chat open in Sprout. Tony’s post appears live.

A parent who gets the push

Aisha, Ava’s mom, isn’t in the app. Her phone is locked until Tony posts.

The sitter

Whoever a parent shares. Messages only, no Sprout. Waits until Tony asks.

How it connects

  1. Tony taps Send to the group. Priya sees his message arrive in the class chat; Aisha’s locked phone gets the push Tony is looking for a sitter and opens the chat at his message.
  2. Either taps Share a sitter, picks people from their contacts, adds a note. Only Tony gets the names and numbers; the chat only shows them “You shared … privately with Tony”.
  3. Tony sees each parent’s sitters as they come in, saved to his sitters, and taps Ask. The first one he asks gets the text on the fourth phone and taps Yes, I’m free.
  4. Tony taps Book. The sitter gets the booking text, the parents hear back in the chat (thanked by name if it was their sitter). Start over on any phone resets all four.
