Both sides of a request

When Sprout asks someone for you (a playdate, a ride, a sitter), this is what they get. On each row the two phones are connected: do it on Tony’s phone on the left, and the person he asks gets it on the right. Their answer, and anything they type, goes back to Tony. Start over on either phone resets both.

Playdate

Live: the two phones are connected. Plan it on Tony’s phone; the parent he picks gets it on the right, in their chat with Tony, and whatever they tap goes back to Tony.

Tony

Picks the kid, the time and the place, then the parent.

→Sprout
sends it

The parent Tony picks

Waits on the lock screen until Tony sends the invite.

How it connects

  1. Tony picks the kid, time and place, then a parent, say Ana and Ben. Sprout messages them.
  2. Their phone gets the push and opens their chat with Tony: Confirm, Suggest another time, Can’t make it or Reply.
  3. Confirm puts it on both calendars. A new time comes back to Tony to Accept or answer with another. Can’t lets Tony ask another family. Anything typed shows on both phones.
  4. Start over on either phone resets both.

Carpool · regular, two families

A regular carpool is two families taking turns. Tony ranks who to ask; Sprout asks one family at a time, in Sprout, and only asks the next if they can’t. The right phone is whoever is being asked: tap Can’t this season and it becomes the next family’s phone.

How it connects

  1. Tony ranks Priya 1, Aisha 2 and taps Ask Priya first.
  2. Priya answers on the right: Can’t this season and Sprout asks Aisha next (the phone becomes hers), or We’re in.
