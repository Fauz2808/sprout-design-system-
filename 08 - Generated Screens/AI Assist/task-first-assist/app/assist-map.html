Sprout Assist: how it wires into the app

Every Assist request becomes one plan. The plan moves through states, and each state writes to the same places: Daily Brief, to-dos, pushes, the family calendar, the spouse, class chat, saved details, and pages outside Sprout. Tap a flow, a state or any chip to see its connections.

The rules I suggest

  1. One plan, many views. A plan is the single source. The Daily Brief, Your plans, the calendar and to-dos only read from it, so they can never disagree.
  2. One push per state change, to whoever has to act. Needs your OK → you (and Dana if either can approve). Booked → both. Nothing to do → no push.
  3. Anything with a time goes on the family calendar, gets the 30-minute push, and an evening out also gets a Live Activity.
  4. Anything the parent must do later becomes a to-do with origin “Sprout Assist”, and is ticked off by Sprout when it’s done (pay Alex, pack for camp week 2).
  5. When a plan ends it updates saved details (sitter sat count, provider accounts, kids’ sizes), so the next plan asks less.
