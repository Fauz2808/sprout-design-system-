Create a club, without the dialogue

A working prototype, not a picture. Hold the microphone and let go whenever you like. What you did not say stays unfilled, and nothing on screen asks you for it.

Tony, 15 Sep: “The club creation has a ton of dialogue. Remember when we're like, okay, ready to begin? Well, I'm like, yes. And now I have to hit the mute button every time when I'm walkie-talking with them. We won't have any of that… I'm just gonna be like, I'm doing this, this, this, and this, and then it'll just spit it out.”

Worth trying in this order

  1. Hold all the way to the end. Every chip turns green, the preview is complete.
  2. Start over, then let go after two seconds. Only the club name was caught. The description and the privacy setting arrive amber, and the assistant still says nothing.
  3. Type over any field. A mishearing is fixed by editing, never by recording again.
Why one flow and not five. Tony has not seen the direction page yet, and in PMF the expensive mistake is building five of something before anyone has held one. Club is the flow he named as the worst offender, so it is the fair test. Once he says the shape is right, the other four are the same three screens with different fields, which is the whole point of the pattern.
