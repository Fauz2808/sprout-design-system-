App Store screenshots · V.6 concepts

Three directions after V.3–V.5 felt flat (5 Nov). Same story as onboarding, in onboarding order. Illustrations are the onboarding panels exported from Figma; headlines are shortened for the store, where people read three slides in a second while scrolling. Scroll to the bottom to compare the three in an App Store search result.

A

Poster: huge type, illustration breaking out

The headline is the hero, sized to read at thumbnail size. Each illustration is oversized, tilted and bleeds off the edge, so every slide feels like a cut-out from the app instead of a picture of a phone. Backgrounds rotate green · cream · deep green so the row has rhythm.

B

Panorama: one continuous story

All six slides are cut from one wide canvas. A cream ground (the onboarding surface) waves through every frame and the dotted path runs across the seams, so swiping feels like walking through a parent's week. Panoramic sets are the pattern that gets people to swipe to the last slide.

C

Stage: onboarding on top, big statement below

The closest to onboarding (illustration on top, explanation below) but pushed for the store: the illustration sits on the cream onboarding stage with a curved edge, and the bottom is a solid green block with a large statement. Phone crops end inside the stage, so nothing is cut in mid-air.

How they read in App Store search (first three slides)
