What the trending apps do

Slide 1 is a character, not a phone.Tolan, Finch and Orbits (“Meet Bit. The little planet that could.”) open with their mascot and one big promise.
The mascot lives in the product.Ohai’s octopus peeks out from behind every phone. Bit sits inside Orbits’ UI. It makes a set feel like one story.
Warm, light backgrounds.Tolan, Orbits, Ohai and Structured all use cream, peach or pastel with dark, friendly headlines and one word in the brand color.
UI breaks out of the phone.Chat bubbles, cards and reminders float over the frame edge (Tolan, Bevel, Ohai), so the point reads at thumbnail size.
Before → after stories.Orbits: “Take the group chat from this… / To this…”, “Go from emails… to actions.” Exactly Sprout’s email-to-to-do promise.
