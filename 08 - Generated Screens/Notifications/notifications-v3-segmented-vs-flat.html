One notifications file, two structural takes on To-do

Screen 1 is what we already shipped: To-do sits as one pill among the others (All / Events / Clubs / Calendar / To-do / Daily Brief), rescued from getting buried by a pinned "Needs your attention" strip. Screen 2 promotes To-do one level up — a 2-way segmented control (Notifications / To-do) right under the header, same idea as the existing Discover / My Events pattern on Home. Both stay inside this one screen — no bottom nav change, no Home change.

Screen 1 — flat tab

To-do as one pill among equals

Goal: minimal structural change — To-do is just another filter next to Events/Clubs/Calendar. The pinned strip does the work of keeping it from getting lost. Trade-off: a first-time user scanning the tab row sees 6 equal-weight pills — nothing tells them To-do is categorically different (actionable) until they notice the pinned strip.

Screen 2 — segmented

To-do promoted to its own segment

Goal: a 2-way segmented control right under the header — "Notifications" and "To-do" are co-equal destinations, not one-pill-among-six. To-do gets the full screen to itself (Open/Completed), no squeezing into a pinned strip. Trade-off: one extra tap to get from a general notification to the to-do list (they're siblings now, not blended) — but the separation reads instantly, no strip needed to explain it.
