Where should to-do live? Two more placements, compared

Baseline so far: to-do split into its own Notifications tab with a pinned "Needs your attention" strip. That fixes the burying problem without touching anything outside Notifications — but it still asks parents to open Notifications to see it. These two options trade that low cost for stronger visibility, at different prices.

Current baseline: pinned strip inside Notifications — see notifications-v2-full-taxonomy.html
Option A

Dedicated "Tasks" tab

Goal: treat to-dos as a different app section entirely — not a feed, a checklist. Always one tap away regardless of notification volume, with its own badge on the nav bar itself (the same visibility tier as Chat's unread count).
Cost: Sprout's tab bar is already full — Home / Discover / Create / Clubs / Chat is 5 slots. Adding Tasks means cramming a 6th icon (shown here, compressed) or replacing an existing tab. That's a bigger IA call than anything scoped to Notifications alone — highest visibility, highest cost to ship.

Option B

"Needs your attention" card on Home

Goal: zero new navigation. Meet parents where they already land every single open — Home — with the 1-2 things that need action, completable inline without opening Notifications at all. Notifications goes back to being a pure passive log.
Cost: only visible if the user actually lands on Home first — someone tapping straight into a chat or a push notification can miss it entirely. And Home currently leads with "discover fun things nearby" (per PRODUCT.md); a chores card at the very top risks a tonal clash right at the app's front door.
