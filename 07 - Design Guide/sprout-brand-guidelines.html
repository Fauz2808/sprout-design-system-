01 — Brand Core

Who We Are

Sprout is the platform where parents find their people — not just playdates, but a genuine community that grows with their family. Every design and word choice should reflect that human warmth.

Brand Idea

"You got lucky when you found these people.
Sprout is where that luck happens."

The Party Line

"You know how when you have kids and move somewhere new you basically have to start your whole social life over? I'm building the app that solves that."

Brand Personality Pillars

🌱
Warm & Human
We speak like a trusted friend, never a corporate product. The tone is always supportive, real, and never preachy.
🍀
Serendipitous
The best connections feel like happy accidents. Our brand celebrates the joy of finding your people — that feeling of "lucky me."
🌳
Grounded & Safe
Parents trust us with what matters most. We feel reliable, calm, and rooted — never flashy, never alarming.

Logo System

Sprout

Sprout
Reversed — on dark background
Icon-only — app icon, favicon

Logo Rules

Always maintain a clear space of at least 1× the icon height around the logo. Never stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects to the logo. The clover icon always appears true to its original form — never replace with a generic emoji or altered version. On dark backgrounds, use the logo at natural opacity. Never use the wordmark without the clover. Minimum display size: 28px height for icon, 80px width for the full lockup.

05 — Voice & Tone

Brand Voice

Sprout sounds like that friend who just moved to your city and immediately made everyone feel welcome. Effortlessly social, real, never trying too hard.

☕
Warm & Genuine
We write like a real person, not a brand. Direct, sincere, and never over-polished. "You know how..." not "Our platform enables..."
🎲
Serendipitous
We celebrate the magic of unexpected connections. Language should feel like a happy coincidence — light, surprising, and lucky.
🌿
Grounded & Calm
No hype. No urgency pressure. Parents are already overwhelmed — Sprout is the exhale, not another notification to worry about.
🤝
Community-first
We always credit the community. "Patrick Collison is hosting" not "an event is available near you." Real names, real places, real people.

✓ We Do Say

  • "You got lucky when you found these people."
  • "James is hosting a surf session Saturday."
  • "2 Meeting Points waiting for you."
  • "Parks and playgrounds near you."
  • "Where that luck happens."
  • "Patrick and 4 others are going."

✗ We Don't Say

  • "Optimize your social network today!"
  • "Find events using our AI-powered engine."
  • "Don't miss out — limited spots!"
  • "Our platform connects families seamlessly."
  • "Sign up to unlock premium features."
  • "Leverage community-driven synergies."

07 — Imagery

Photography & Visual Style

Sprout imagery should feel documentary, not staged. Real families, natural light, genuine moments. The camera is a witness to joy, not a director of it.

🏞️
✓ Use: Natural & Candid Kids playing, parents laughing, outdoor activities in natural light. Warm tones, real moments, community gatherings.
📸
✗ Avoid: Over-produced Stock photo smiles, studio lighting, perfect outfits, overly bright & saturated edits. Never sterile or corporate.

Image Treatment

Event thumbnails use a 1:1 crop at 80×80px with 12–16px border radius. Hero images use a 16:9 or 3:2 ratio with slight warm overlay (rgba(240,237,230,0.1)). Images in cards always pair with Playfair Display titles overlaid below, never on top. Always maintain cream/warm color harmony — cool-tinted or blue-cast photography should be avoided or warmed up.

Motion Principles

Motion in Sprout should feel like nature — a leaf settling, not a loading spinner. Use spring physics for card interactions (slight overshoot then settle). Never use jarring cuts or aggressive bounces. Celebration moments (joining an event, meeting someone new) deserve a gentle scale + fade. Keep all animations under 400ms. Respect prefers-reduced-motion at all times.

Grid Rules

Maintain a warm, earthy tone — no cold blue casts. Alternate between real community moments, Forest Green quote cards, Cream quote cards, and app screen previews. Every 3rd post should feature a branded graphic with Playfair Display italic quote on Forest Green. Add the Sprout clover watermark (bottom-right, 10% opacity overlay) to all original content posts.

Caption Voice

✓ Caption Do's

  • Lead with a human moment, not a product feature
  • Keep under 150 words for feed posts
  • Soft CTA: "Join us →" or "Sound like your people?"
  • Use first-person plural: "we", "our community"
  • Name real people and real places when possible
  • End with 5–8 targeted hashtags on a separate line

✗ Caption Don'ts

  • "SIGN UP NOW" or "LIMITED TIME OFFER"
  • More than 8 hashtags — looks spammy
  • Generic mega-tags like #parenting (too broad)
  • Emoji spam — max 3 emojis per caption
  • Promotional language about features or pricing
  • Tagging unrelated accounts for reach

✓ Deck Do's

  • One clear idea per slide — no cramming
  • Generous white/cream space on every slide
  • Sprout logo bottom-right on every content slide (28px height)
  • Playfair Display for emotional headlines and quotes
  • Natural, candid photos only (never stock smiles)
  • Charts in Sprout green palette exclusively
  • Section divider slides between major chapters

✗ Deck Don'ts

  • More than 5 bullet points on any slide
  • Drop shadows or gradients on slide backgrounds
  • Off-brand colors (purple, blue, red, orange)
  • More than 2 typefaces in a single deck
  • Cluttered data slides — simplify your charts first
  • Centering body text (left-align always)
  • Comic Sans, Papyrus, or decorative fonts
