## 1. Both sides of a request: the phones now talk

**Before:** the right phone played a fixed script. It showed Ana even when Tony messaged David.

**Now:** each row is one live conversation. The right phone waits on the lock screen, gets Tony's real request (his
kids, time, place, message) and becomes whoever he asked. Their taps go back to Tony; his answers come back to them.
Typed messages show on both phones. Start over resets both.

| Row | What the other person can do |

| Playdate | Confirm, suggest a new time (Tony accepts or counters), can't make it (Tony asks the next family, the phone becomes theirs) |
| Carpool, team chat | I can drive, or "Can't at 9:30" plus the times they could; Tony moves the pickup |
| Carpool, parents Tony picks | Same ride card, now in a Sprout DM (see 3) |
| Regular carpool | Can't this season (the phone becomes the next family's), We're in, then the shared turns |

## 4. Getting a sitter paid

The biggest block of work. One rule throughout: **Sprout first** (Stripe, tracked), other apps allowed.

**4a. The payout link** (`/pay/`), texted right after a booking. One decision per screen:

1. The booking (Tony booked you, date, kids, about $100), then **Add your bank or card**, or "I'd rather use
   Venmo, Zelle or cash".
2. Stripe: bank account (1–2 days, no fee) or debit card (minutes, small fee).
3. "Anything else families can use?" (optional): Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, PayPal, Cash.
4. "You're set", with where each payment goes.

Commits `47b1d87`, `1099bae` (the first version stacked two pickers on one screen and confused which to do first).

**4b. How each app connects** (commit `bfd942a`):

| App | How | Why |
|---|---|---|
| PayPal | **Connect with PayPal**: sign in, approve, connected (like Upwork) | PayPal offers a real "Log in with PayPal" connect |
| Venmo, Zelle | One tap: the sitter's number from the text | Neither lets other apps sign in; both pay to a phone number |
| Cash App | The sitter types their $cashtag | No sign-in for other apps |
| Cash | Nothing to set up | |

**4c. What Tony sees** (commits `d9f2be4`, `5733f77`):
- Booked card: "Waiting on Alex to add where to get paid", then a note and a ping when she's done, and "Alex takes
  Sprout, Venmo and Cash". Tony never sees bank details, only "bank account" or "debit card".
- Pay card: **Pay Alex $112** in Sprout, or "Or pay Alex another way" with exactly her apps. An app opens with the
  amount; cash is marked paid. Either way Sprout texts her, then the rating.
- **Once per sitter:** pay info lives on the sitter's own Sprout account. A sitter already set up (Sam in the demo)
  gets no link: "Sam is already set up to get paid".
