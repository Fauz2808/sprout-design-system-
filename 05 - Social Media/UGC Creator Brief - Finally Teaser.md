# Sprout — "Finally." Video Ad Creator Brief

**Campaign:** "Finally." teaser
**Format:** Vertical social video (screen-recording + text-bubble + short lifestyle b-roll)
**Target length:** 45–48 sec hero cut, with 15s and 30s cutdowns
**Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels/Feed, Facebook
**Brand:** Sprout — "Where parents bond and childhoods flourish."

---

## 1. What this ad actually needs (read this first)

This is **not** a talking-head UGC script — nobody talks to camera. It's a hybrid:
1. **Text-message chaos** — animated chat bubbles on a phone screen (motion graphics / text-story style)
2. **Real app screen recordings** — Sprout's actual UI (calendar reminders, birthday invite flow, sharing to groups)
3. **~10 sec of lifestyle b-roll** — one mom talent, two short unscripted-feeling moments (frantic → calm)
4. **Voiceover** — a single warm, slightly wry VO carries the narration throughout

So you need to brief three different people/vendors: a **VO artist**, a **mom talent** for b-roll only, and an **editor/motion designer** to assemble it. If sourcing through a UGC platform (Billo, Trend, Insense), split this into a "lifestyle b-roll" order (talent) and handle the text-bubble/screen-recording assembly separately with an editor — most UGC creators aren't set up to build animated chat-bubble graphics.

## 4. Mom talent brief (b-roll only, ~10 sec total on camera)

- **Casting:** parent, naturally warm/relatable on camera, comfortable with two moods — frazzled and settled. No line delivery required, no dialogue, no lip sync.
- **Scene A (0:09–0:13):** genuinely looks like she's toggling between two apps trying to find an invite — slightly stressed, phone close to face, quick thumb movements. ~3–4 sec, can be shot as several takes/angles for the editor to choose from.
- **Scene B (0:43–0:48):** sitting on a couch, coffee mug in hand, phone in the other. Two notification pop-ups appear (added in post) and she reacts with a small, genuine smile — not a big grin, just relief/calm. ~5 sec.
- **Wardrobe/setting:** casual homewear, natural light, lived-in living room. Avoid busy patterns or any competing brand logos in frame.
- **Do NOT** have her say the brand name, read any script lines aloud, or address the camera — this is silent b-roll only, VO is added separately.

---

## 5. Voiceover brief

- **Read:** warm, dry-witted, knowing — like a friend telling you "I get it" before pivoting to "but here's the fix." Not corporate, not hyper-peppy.
- **Pacing:** faster/clipped through the chaos beats (1–3), a clear beat of silence at 0:14–0:17, then calmer and more confident from the logo reveal onward.
- **Full VO script:**
  1. "Every parent has this group chat."
  2. "The one where nobody knows who's who… and nobody can find anything."
  3. "And somehow every birthday invite lives in a different app."
  4. "There has to be a better way."
  5. "Meet Sprout. The app built for the village it actually takes to raise a kid."
  6. "Know exactly who you're talking to. Upload your school calendar once and everyone gets reminders. Send birthday invitations right from Sprout — even to people outside your usual group. Organize camps, sports, and playdates all in one place."
  7. "Because parenting already comes with enough tabs open."
  8. "Less chaos. More community."
