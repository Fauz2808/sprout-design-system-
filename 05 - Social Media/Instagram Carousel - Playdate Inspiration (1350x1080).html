Step 01
01
Start with a
signature snack

Before the chaos begins, greet kids with one simple, consistent snack. It signals "we're welcome here" and gives everyone a calm moment to land.

💡
Keep it the same every time — kids talk about "the house with the cinnamon popcorn." Become that house.
Sprout
3 / 7
Step 02
02
Have one activity
ready. Not five.

A simple starter activity — craft, LEGO, playdough — gives kids something to do while parents settle in. It's a launchpad, not a schedule.

💡
Resist the urge to over-plan. One activity is enough to break the ice. Kids will take it from there.
Sprout
4 / 7
Step 03
03
Then step back
and let them
free-play

The magic moment of every playdate isn't the activity — it's what happens after. Free play builds creativity, social skills, and the friendships that last.

💡
Your job during free play: make yourself a coffee and actually enjoy it. You've earned it.
Sprout
5 / 7
Step 04
04
Create a
little tradition
just for you two

A small ritual — a goodbye high-five, a special playlist, a drawing wall — turns a one-off visit into something kids look forward to every single time.

💡
It doesn't have to be elaborate. "We always draw one picture before we leave" is enough to make it memorable.
Sprout
6 / 7
Step 05
05
End 15 minutes
before you
have to

The secret weapon. Wrap up while energy is still high. Kids leave on a win — not a meltdown — and immediately start asking when they can come back.

💡
"One more thing, then we'll go" is a trap. Set a 10-minute warning and stick to it. Future you will be grateful.
