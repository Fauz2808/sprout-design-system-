No playdate to schedule, no favour to ask. You just saw something funny and their name popped into your head. That shift — from transactional to genuine — is when a parent contact becomes a parent friend.

💬
The "saw this and thought of you" text is the adult version of passing notes in class. It means you made it.

You've stopped hovering at the door. You're not running a mental checklist of what could go wrong. Your kid runs in and you actually get to breathe — because you trust who's on the other side.

🚪
That moment you drive away without checking your phone every five minutes? That's a village moment. Hold onto those families.

Not the clean version. The actual "we just got home and someone's crying" version. When you stop tidying before they arrive and they stop apologising for their kids — that's when it gets real.

😅
Real parent friendship happens the first time one of you admits the day has been a disaster. That honesty is the whole foundation.

Plans

Not "we should do something" for the fourth week in a row. An actual date, an actual place. When someone in your circle finally stops talking and starts doing — and the rest follow — you've got something worth keeping.

🗓️
The person who always picks the date and books the venue? They're holding your village together. Tell them you appreciate it.
