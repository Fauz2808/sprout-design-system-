Design Decisions
Subtitle trimmed on every screen — Long opening paragraphs removed. Each screen now has 1 headline + 1 supporting line max. "We'll personalise events, clubs, and activities to match each of your child's information" → gone. Form fields are self-explanatory.
4.1/4.2 warm background retained — The photo overlay is the best brand moment in onboarding. v2 keeps it but shifts the subtitle from a marketing tagline ("Weekend play dates begin here") to a functional cue ("Tell us a bit about yourself.").
Add Child modal: "Tell us about your kid" removed — The modal title "Add a Child" already explains what this is. The subtitle under it was repeating the same message. Replaced with a compact "STEP 1 OF 2" pill so users know progress exists without over-explaining.
4.3/4.14 void addressed — The original 42–64px gap created a hollow lower half. The empty state and kid cards now sit in a tight content stack. The remaining whitespace is natural breathing room, not structural emptiness.
4.16 school list void filled with a tip card — Below 5 school rows there were ~180px of nothing. Added a contextual note: "School groups connect you with parents nearby." This fills the void while delivering genuine value (answering the implicit "why should I do this?" question).
"Join your kids school groups" → "Schools near you" — The original headline was imperative and long. v2 uses a factual label — the zip badge provides location context so the headline doesn't need to repeat it. Shorter + less demanding tone.
4.17 grade picker inline — Instead of a separate modal for grade selection, the school row expands in-place. Reduces navigation depth and keeps the user in context of the full school list.
4.19 AI screen: mic button italic — "Tap to Talk with AI" → italicised Playfair. Shifts the register from instruction to invitation. The pulsing ring animation (CSS @keyframes) reinforces that the button is alive and responsive.
4.20 AI question subtitle halved — Original: "What activities do you personally enjoy doing in your free time? Any sports, hobbies, of food preferences worth sharing with other parents" (28 words) → v2: "Tell us about sports, hobbies, or food preferences worth sharing." (10 words). Same information, half the scan time.
Botanical accent consistent throughout — Corner sprig at 12% opacity on every screen that has dead space. Used as texture only — it never competes with the primary content. AI screens omit it entirely since the dotted sphere and mic button are already visually rich.
