Design decisions
Headline reframed — "Permission Required" (cold & clinical) → "Just two quick things" / "One more to go" (warm, progress-oriented). Changes the emotional register from demand to guidance.
288px void resolved — Permission rows grew into cards with names, reasons, and actions. Cards + trust note + CTA fill the space purposefully; no padding hacks needed.
Inline "Why" — Each permission shows a one-line reason directly beneath the name. Users don't need to parse the subtitle to understand what each permission does.
"Allow All" surfaced — Moved from a low-opacity top-right pill to the full-width primary CTA. Confident users take one tap; cautious users allow individually from each card.
Trust note — Single line with shield icon: "Your data never leaves your device or is sold." Addresses the unstated fear without adding a second paragraph of copy.
Context-aware headline on 3.2 — When one permission is done, the headline shifts to "One more to go" — acknowledging progress and reducing cognitive load for the remaining step.
Botanical texture only — Corner sprig at 12% opacity. No full-width illustration scene. Permissions is a trust moment; the design stays clean and focused, not decorative.
