# SPROUT — "Finally." — Voiceover Script (clean read)

For recording or TTS (MiniMax / ElevenLabs / ChatGPT voice). One voice throughout — a parent, not an announcer. Tone shifts from tired/wry to warm/confident exactly once, at the Scene 4 turn.

Read at a natural conversational pace, not ad-read pace. Leave the pauses marked — they're doing narrative work.

---

**[Scenes 1–2 — tired, deadpan, slightly exasperated. Not panicked-yelling, more "I cannot believe this is my life."]**

> Who is 512-304-...? Is that Mason's mom? Or Lily's?
>
> Wait — is today free dress or not?!
>
> Anyone? ...Anyone??

**[Scene 3 — silence. No line. Let the beat sit for a full second.]**

**[Scene 4 — tone flips here. Slower, warmer, quietly confident. This is the whole ad's pivot point — don't rush it.]**

> There's a better way.

**[Scene 5 — same warm register, a little more energy under it]**

> Meet Sprout. The app built for the village it actually takes to raise a kid.

**[Scenes 6–10 — clean, confident, benefit-forward. Each line is its own complete thought — leave a breath between them, don't run them together even if you're cutting fast visually.]**

> Real profiles. Real names. Real faces — so you actually know who you're talking to.
>
> Upload your school calendar once. Sprout sends the whole group a reminder the morning before — free dress, library day, early pickup — handled.
>
> iMessage drops Android users. WhatsApp has zero community. Sprout was built for all of you.
>
> Find families with the same interests. Set up playdates. See which camps and sports teams your kids' friends are signing up for.
>
> One organized parent updates the group — everyone benefits.

**[Scene 11 — softest, most relaxed line in the whole script. This is the exhale.]**

> Less chaos. More community.

**[Scene 12 — same warmth as Scene 4, land it flat and simple, no upsell energy]**

> Sprout. Finally, a group chat that actually works.

---

## Cut-down lines (if trimming to 30s per Tony's note)

Drop entirely if tightening: the iMessage/WhatsApp line (Scene 8) and the camps/sports half of the Scene 9 line (keep "Find families with the same interests. Set up playdates," cut "See which camps and sports teams your kids' friends are signing up for.").

## On-screen text (for muted autoplay — pair 1:1 with the VO above)

Every line above should also appear as burned-in caption text, styled once and reused:
- Font: Playfair Display (headline weight) for the punchy short lines (Scene 1, 4, 12), Inter for anything longer/list-like (Scenes 6–10).
- Color: `#2a2e25` (near-black) text on light shots, `#f4f1ea` (cream) text on dark/kitchen shots — never brand-green text on cream, it under-contrasts at caption size.
- One line on screen at a time, synced to the VO breath groups already marked above — don't cram multi-sentence VO lines into a single caption card.
