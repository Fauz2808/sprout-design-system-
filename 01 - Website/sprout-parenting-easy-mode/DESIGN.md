# Design direction

## Concept: calm operating system for family life

This variation follows the leadership brief with an editorial, calm, high-trust layout. It moves from the daily problem to product proof, community, weekends, and the founder-level closing statement.

## Flow

1. Parenting on Easy Mode hero with interactive phone-in-hand preview.
2. Daily Brief walkthrough: Reminder, Invitation, Happening Today, Upcoming.
3. Class Hub: chat, calendars, teacher emails, and important links.
4. Voice AI: Reminders, Birthdays, Events, and Carpool.
5. Parent Directory with private in-app messaging.
6. Weekend Planner with local events and group sharing.
7. Social/belonging and communication/reminder pain points.
8. Closing founder statement and store links.

## Visual system

- Newsreader for warm editorial display type.
- DM Sans for compact product UI and body copy.
- Sprout green with cream, pale blue, sage, and peach section fields.
- Floating glass navigation, rounded product stages, restrained reveal motion.
- Product screens use supplied assets; missing imagery is labeled as a placeholder.

## Motion

- Hero preview swaps between Daily Brief, Invitations, and Class Chat.
- Daily Brief walkthrough cycles automatically and remains directly clickable.
- Voice AI tabs update prompt and result in place.
- Scroll reveals respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
