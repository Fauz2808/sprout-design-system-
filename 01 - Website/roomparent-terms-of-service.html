2. What Room Parent is

Room Parent is a free, web-based tool for the volunteers who run a classroom. It helps you keep a class calendar, publish a school lunch menu, draft and send class emails, collect volunteer sign-ups and consent forms, share links, track a class fund, and split a checklist with your co-room-parent.

Room Parent is independent. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any school, school district, or parent-teacher organization. Using it does not create any relationship between us and your school.

Families do not need an account or an app to take part. They receive your emails and open your links the way they always have.

3. Who can use it

  • You should be a room parent, class volunteer, or someone the class or teacher has asked to help run it.
  • Access is granted per class. Do not sign in to or use a class you have not been given access to.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Follow your school's rules about handling family and student information, including any rules about rosters, photos, and directories.

  • Keep entries accurate — particularly class fund records, which other room parents and families may rely on.
