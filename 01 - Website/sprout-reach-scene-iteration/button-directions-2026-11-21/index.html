Direction ASoft glow pill

Closest to Timeleft, the site most like ours. A warm white pill that breathes wider on hover while a soft mint light rises inside it. The dull icons are replaced by the official store badges under “or get the app”.

  • From: Timeleft (widening), Bevel (rising light, 1.025 spring)
  • Strength: instantly familiar for parents, badges are crisp and official
  • Watch: two rows of things to read under the lede

Direction BArrow handoff

The agency move. A white pill with a small green circle at the end holding a dot. On hover the green floods the whole pill, the label turns white and the dot hands over to an arrow. Beside it, a frosted “Get the app” pill with crisp, full-white Apple and Android marks.

  • From: Lusion (dot → arrow), Stripe (arrow that grows)
  • Strength: the most crafted feel, one clean row
  • Watch: Apple’s guidelines prefer the official badge over the bare Apple logo

Chosen (Ahmad, 21 Nov): B’s button + A’s store badges

The arrow handoff for “Join Sprout”, with “or get the app” and the official App Store and Google Play badges under it. Built into home-v3 as .btn-hand (home-v3.css). B’s frosted “Get the app” pill was too dull.

Claude’s first pick was C, with A’s badges on phones

C is the only one that feels like Sprout rather than borrowed: the app icon in the button answers “is this an app?” without the dull grey glyphs, and the QR card turns a desktop visit into an install on the spot. On a phone the QR card hides and the button goes straight to the store.

If you want the safest choice for Tony’s parents, A. If you want the most “agency” feel, B.
