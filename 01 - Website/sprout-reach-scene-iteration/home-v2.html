Questions parents ask

Is Sprout free?

Yes. Sprout is free for parents. Always.

What if my school isn't on Sprout yet?

Get Sprout and ask for your school from the app. The fastest way to bring your whole class is your room parent: they can set the class up on RoomParent in about 5 minutes, and every parent they invite lands in the same class on Sprout.

Who can see my kids?

No kid profiles. No public posts. Only verified parents at your school can see your class, and your phone number and email stay private until you choose to share them.

What does Sprout read from my email?

Only the senders you choose, like your teacher and your room parent. Nothing else. You can turn a sender off at any time.

Will Sprout Assist pay for things or see my passwords?

Nothing is paid or sent without your yes. Before checkout it shows you what, who it's for and how much, and your spouse sees who approved it. When a site asks you to sign in, Sprout pauses and hands it to you. It never sees or saves your password.

Can my spouse see the same things?

Invite them from your Daily Brief. Once they join, you share one Family To-Do List, the same invitations and the same calendar, and you both see who already did what.
