Behaviour to keep

  • Pinned steps (watchSteps / updateSteps in home-v2.js): section height = 100vh + units × 70vh, the inner wrapper is position: sticky. Progress through the section maps to a step by weight (data-w, default 1). All steps sit in one grid cell so the box never changes height. Do not go back to text scrolling past a sticky phone.
  • Daily Brief scrub (scrubBrief, drawShared, glide): everything is driven by scroll position, eased toward it each frame, so scrolling back up plays it backwards.
  • Phone screens are code, not images, built at the app's 393 × 852 and scaled with --sa-scale. Keep them as components.
  • Store links: every data-join link goes to the App Store; on Android the script swaps in Google Play.
  • Phone width (≤ 900px): headline, phone and step copy fit one screen; only the step copy swaps.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: no glide, no fades, portal unpinned.
