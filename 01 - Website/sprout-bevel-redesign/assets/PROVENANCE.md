## Original family image generation prompt

Create one candid editorial lifestyle photograph of several diverse parents and young children naturally connecting at a relaxed neighborhood park picnic. Place the scene in a leafy residential neighborhood park with three parents and four children interacting naturally on a picnic blanket. Use a landscape 3:2 composition, keeping the people concentrated in the left and middle of the frame with calmer visual space on the right for a product interface overlay. The image should feel like high-end documentary lifestyle photography, warm and human but not staged, with natural late-afternoon light, honest skin texture, subdued clothing, and a premium editorial color grade. No text, logos, watermark, posed group portrait, exaggerated smiles, artificial depth of field, or glossy stock-photo styling.
