# Sprout landing page options

## Option 1: Initial Bevel interpretation

Path: `sprout-bevel-redesign/`

Editorial serif typography, broad family photography, calm green brand system, and an earlier interpretation of Bevel's spacing and floating navigation.

## Option 2: Neighborhood Noticeboard

Path: `sprout-antislop-redesign/`

Warm community-first direction inspired by local events and invitation products. More expressive typography, noticeboard objects, and stronger family character.

## Option 3: Connected Family System

Path: `sprout-bevel-redesign-v2/`

Current requested direction. Closest to Bevel's visual language through a centered atmospheric hero, floating capsule navigation, large sans typography, real product screens, pastel product slabs, and a focused dark profile section. All content and brand signals remain specific to Sprout.

## Option 4: Connected Family System, Side-by-Side

Path: `sprout-bevel-side-by-side/`

The same Bevel-led visual system and Sprout content as Option 3, with a product-led desktop hero. Copy and CTA sit on the left while a nearly complete Sprout home screen is presented at a readable size on the right. Mobile returns to the centered vertical composition.
