## Verification

Inspected section screenshots at readable sizes, then the complete desktop/mobile frames. Structural checks covered text bounds, sibling text intersections, fixed text heights, section intersections, image fills, and phone rotation. Result: no detected violations across 131 text layers and 23 named image layers. The two page frames contain 54 Auto Layout frames in total, including copy groups and sections.

## Editing limitations

Text, layout, vector icons, colors, and containers are native Figma layers. Product screens, store badges, logos, and photography are image fills. Screen contents are not editable app components. Browser animations and interactive states are not imported. This is a repaired Figma reconstruction rather than a pixel-exact HTML conversion.
