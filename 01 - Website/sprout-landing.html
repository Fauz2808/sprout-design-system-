Questions

Things parents
always ask

Is Sprout free to use? ▾

Yes — Sprout is completely free to download and use. There's no paywall, no subscription, and no hidden fees. We believe every family deserves access to a strong community.

Is it safe? Who can see my profile? ▾

Safety is our top priority. Your profile is only visible to other verified Sprout members in your area. We verify every account and moderate all community spaces. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

I'm new to the area — is Sprout still useful? ▾

Absolutely — actually, Sprout is built for exactly that situation. Families who just moved are our fastest-growing segment. Our AI helps you find the right people and events from day one, without having to already know anyone.

What if there's no one in my neighbourhood yet? ▾

We're growing fast — and the earlier you join, the more you help Sprout grow in your area. You can also be the first to create a club or host an event, and invite nearby parents to join. Many of our most active communities were started by one parent who took the first step.

Do I need to add my kids' information? ▾

Adding your kids (ages, interests) is optional but recommended — it's how we personalise your event suggestions. You control exactly what information is visible and to whom. We never show specific details about your children publicly.

Is Sprout available on Android? ▾

Currently Sprout is available on iOS (iPhone). Android is on our roadmap — join the waitlist to be notified the moment it launches.
