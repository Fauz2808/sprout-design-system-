# Room Parent Logo V3

## Core idea

An adult and child lean toward one shared opening. The two bodies become one connected structure, representing parents helping create a dependable space for a classroom community.

## Construction system

- Golden ratio constant: `φ = 1.618`
- Child head radius: `60`
- Adult head radius: `97.08`, equal to `60 × 1.618`
- Child body-width module: `148.33`
- Adult body-width module: `240`, equal to approximately `148.33 × 1.618`
- Both figures sit on one shared baseline.
- Shoulders, arms, inner opening, and lower corners use tangent circular curves.
- The mark uses optical centering rather than mirror symmetry. The larger adult mass is counterbalanced by the child's position on the right.

## Improvements from the selected V2 direction

- Reduced visual weight and removed the stiff vertical-bar feeling.
- Replaced the sharp hand-meeting point with a smoother shared bridge.
- Standardized head, body, and corner proportions.
- Aligned the feet to one baseline.
- Replaced the heavy wordmark with a medium humanist sans serif.
- Preserved the adult-child relationship at 64 px and 32 px.

## Usage status

This is a review candidate. The logo has not replaced the current website or portal identity.
