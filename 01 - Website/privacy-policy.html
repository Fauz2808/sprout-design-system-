Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

At Sprout, accessible from www.joinsprout.co, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our community members. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we collect and how we use it to help parents find their people and build genuine connections that last.

This policy applies only to our online activities and is valid for visitors to our website and users of the Sprout app, with regards to the information they share and/or that we collect. It is not applicable to any information collected offline or via channels other than Sprout.

Consent

By using Sprout, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Information We Collect

The personal information that you are asked to provide, and the reasons why you are asked to provide it, will be made clear to you at the point we ask you to provide it.

If you contact us directly, we may receive additional information about you such as your name, email address, phone number, the contents of the message and/or attachments you may send us, and any other information you may choose to provide.

When you register for an account, we may ask for your contact information, including items such as name, phone number, neighborhood or general location, and details about your family that help us connect you with the right community.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

Provide & operate

Run and maintain the Sprout app and website.

Personalize

Connect you with families and communities near you.

Improve

Understand how you use Sprout so we can keep improving it.

Communicate

Send support, updates, and information about events near you.

Notify

Send you relevant notifications and emails.

Keep safe

Prevent fraud and protect our community.

Log Files

Sprout follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit our website and app. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the platform, tracking usage patterns, and gathering demographic information to better serve our community.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Sprout uses cookies. These cookies are used to store information including your preferences and the pages on our website that you accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize your experience by customizing our web page content based on your browser type and/or other information.

Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

You may consult this list to find the Privacy Policy for each of Sprout's advertising partners. Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Sprout, which are sent directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.

Note that Sprout has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

Third Party Privacy Policies

Sprout's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party services for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

Information Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share your mobile opt-in data (i.e., your consent to receive communications on your mobile device) with any third parties. Your mobile opt-in data is securely stored and used solely by Sprout to manage your communication preferences.

🔒 We ensure that all third parties with whom we share personal information comply with our privacy standards and are bound by confidentiality agreements to protect your personal information.

CCPA Privacy Rights

Under the CCPA, among other rights, California consumers have the right to:

Request disclosure

Ask us to disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data we've collected about you.

Request deletion

Ask us to delete any personal data about you that we've collected.

Opt out of sale

Request that we do not sell your personal data.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

Right to access

Request copies of your personal data. We may charge a small fee for this service.

Right to rectification

Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.

Right to erasure

Request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.

Right to restrict

Request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.

Right to object

Object to our processing of your personal data.

Data portability

Request that we transfer your data to another organization, or directly to you.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you.

Children's Information

Protecting children online is something we take seriously at Sprout. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their children's online activity.

While Sprout is designed for parents and adults, we do not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided this kind of information on our platform, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will make every effort to promptly remove such information from our records.

Questions about your privacy?

We're happy to help. Reach out and we'll respond within one month.
