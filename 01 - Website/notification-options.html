How they differ

🟢 Live Activity

  • ＋ Fully designed — weather icon, H/L, and at-a-glance counts (birthday / events / to-dos)
  • ＋ Persists on the lock screen; a clear "See your daily brief" button
  • ＋ Feels like Sprout "wakes up with you" in the morning
  • － Heavier — Tony's worry: fatigue if it appears every single day
  • － More to build (ActivityKit)

⚪ Plain notification

  • ＋ Familiar, lightweight, easy to dismiss
  • ＋ Still shows the weather (icon + H/L) + a tap-through to the brief
  • ＋ Simplest to ship
  • － Limited to icon + text — no custom layout or counts
  • － Disappears once dismissed

Both tap straight into the daily brief. Open question for the team: does a daily Live Activity become fatiguing, or is the richer morning moment worth it?
