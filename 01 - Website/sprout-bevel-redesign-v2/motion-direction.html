Motion direction

Four moments worth animating, and six sections deliberately left alone. Every panel replays on demand, because motion cannot be judged from a still.

Already built: 20 scroll reveals Day switcher animates "Try one tap" demo reduced-motion respected
The problem is not too little motion, it is motion with no hierarchy. All twenty reveals use the same 650ms fade and the same 1.5rem rise. When everything moves with equal weight, nothing reads as important, and a page of equal-weight motion is exactly what makes a site feel like a template. So this adds almost no new motion. It varies the weight of what is already there, and puts real craft in four places.
Check this too: every moment degrades to an instant, static state.
