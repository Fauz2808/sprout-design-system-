Phone hardware proportion width 78mm to height 163.4mm. Keep the SAME hand, realistic skin, exact upright front-on pose, lighting, grip, and image canvas 1024x1536. Keep device nearly exactly in original location: top at y129, bottom y957, center x508, width about395px; do not tilt. Screen stays completely blank flat warm ivory, no UI, no text. Include small black Dynamic Island at screen top center, around y170. Background must be genuinely TRANSPARENT alpha with clean cutout edges, no background, no halo or glow. Keep hand forearm coming out bottom-right. Preserve the position, scale and anatomy of fingers. Do not add props or text. This asset will have live website UI composited over its blank screen.
